Newvelles · redesign brief

The problem isn't the search. It's that 672 articles arrive as 179 near-identical piles.

I read the repo, the redesign plan and a real latest_news.json. In that file, the Trump–Putin Alaska summit occupies at least a dozen top-level groupings. The same BBC video about the two leaders meeting appears in eight of them. The tag phrases the clustering produces — [next time], [6 takeaways], [moment trump] — are headline fragments, not topics. So the page reads as noise even though the underlying corpus is small and coherent: roughly 30–40 real stories on a given day.

What I'm assuming

  • A story is the unit, not a grouping. Overlapping clusters merge into one story; an article belongs to exactly one. An LLM names it in five to eight words, once per fetch — a few hundred short calls a day, cents.
  • Momentum comes from the archive you already have. S3 files back to 2021 means a story can carry a day count and a shape without any new collection.
  • Everything stays precomputed and static. No queries at read time: the pipeline writes today.json plus a small rolling momentum.json. The Lightsail micro instance keeps serving one file per view.
  • Search stays, demoted. Browsing leads; the search field filters stories, sources and headlines in place rather than being the front door.
  • Anonymous, no accounts. Nothing here needs a login or stored identity.

The system these three share

Story headline
Generated, sentence case, no clickbait. The old bracket tags survive as secondary keyword pills.
Weight = source count
How many distinct outlets carry it, not how many articles. Twelve outlets beats twenty Yahoo reposts.
Momentum mark
A seven-day shape in the accent — the one place chroma is spent, as a line, never a fill.
One level of depth
Story → its articles. The three-level drilldown collapses into an expand-in-place row.

Lead-story headlines and sources below are real, taken from the sample fetch of 16 August 2025. Secondary rows are placeholder content of realistic shape. Everything is styled on Nocturne tokens.
